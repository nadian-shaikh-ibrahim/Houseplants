Introduction to Houseplants

Houseplants are plants that grow indoors in places like residences and offices. Beyond their aesthetic appeal, they play a crucial role in purifying indoor air and reducing stress.


Core Aspects of Houseplants

1- Popular Plant Types

  • Succulents & Cacti
  • Indoor Foliage (Pothos, Monsteras)
  • Flowering Houseplants (Orchids)
  • Air-Purifying Plants (Snake Plant)

2- Main Care Elements

  1. Lighting - Balancing indirect sunlight
  2. Watering - Ensuring proper drainage
  3. Humidity - Maintaining indoor climate

3- Pro Care Tips & Troubleshooting

  1. Pruning - Removing dead or yellowing leaves
  2. Repotting - Upgrading pots as plants grow
  3. Pest Control - Checking regularly for common insects
